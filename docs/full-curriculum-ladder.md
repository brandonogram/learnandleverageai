## KEY HORMOZI PRINCIPLES EMBEDDED IN THIS CURRICULUM

1. **"Give away the secrets, sell the implementation."** The free session teaches WHAT and WHY. Paid products deliver HOW and DONE-FOR-YOU.

2. **"Those who pay most, pay most attention."** Each level costs more and delivers more. Higher-paying students get more 1-on-1 time, more accountability, and better results.

3. **"Bonuses > Discounts."** Never lower the price. Stack bonuses. The Leadership Program includes $2,155 in bonuses. The 10% same-day discount is the ONLY discount, and it's time-limited.

4. **"Scarcity must be real."** 10-person cap on the Intensive is REAL (can't give 1-on-1 attention to more). 15-person cohort for Leadership is REAL. Monthly workshop frequency is REAL.

5. **"Each product should reveal the next problem."** The free session reveals they can't get consistent results (buy Level 1). Level 1 reveals they need ongoing support (buy Level 2). Level 2 reveals they want to build systems (buy Level 3). Level 3 reveals they want to lead teams (buy Level 4). Level 4 reveals they need someone to build it (buy Level 5/6).

6. **"Client-financed acquisition."** The free session costs nothing to deliver (venue + printing = $300). If it generates even $5K in same-day upsells, every future free session is funded by the last one.

7. **"Status drives desire."** Every level sells a STATUS INCREASE:
   - Level 0: "I went to an AI workshop" (curious)
   - Level 1: "I use AI at work" (capable)
   - Level 2: "I use AI every day" (proficient)
   - Level 3: "I built AI systems" (advanced)
   - Level 4: "I lead AI for my team" (leader)
   - Level 5: "AI runs my department" (executive)
