## 1. NEEDS ANALYSIS

### Who is the audience?

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Age | 45-60 |
| Role | Mid-to-senior corporate professionals — analysts, managers, directors, VPs |
| Industries | Finance/banking (JPMorgan, Capital One, Barclays), pharma (DuPont, AstraZeneca), tech (CSC/DXC), manufacturing (W.L. Gore, Chemours) |
| AI experience | Near zero. Most have heard of ChatGPT but never used it, or tried it once and didn't know what to do |
| AI Skill Self-Assessment (expected average) | 1.5 - 3.0 on a 1-10 scale |
| Mindset | Anxious. They're being told to learn AI by their employer. They feel behind. They're worried about job security. |
| Learning style | Hands-on. They want to DO, not listen to lectures. They want to walk out with something working. |
| Tech comfort | Comfortable with email, Office, Zoom. NOT comfortable with terminal, coding, or installing software. |

### What problem does this training solve?

These professionals hear about AI every day — in meetings, on LinkedIn, in company memos — but have no practical skills. Their companies expect them to "use AI" but haven't provided real training. They feel:
- **Behind:** Younger colleagues seem to "get it" and they don't
- **Overwhelmed:** Too many tools, too much hype, no clear starting point
- **Anxious:** Worried AI will replace them or they'll look incompetent
- **Frustrated:** They tried ChatGPT once, got a bad result, and gave up

This workshop transforms them from anxious observers into confident practitioners in one morning.

### What should they be able to DO after the training?

Not "understand" — DO. Specifically:
1. Open an AI tool and write a useful prompt for their actual job
2. Use the RACE framework to get consistently good results from any AI tool
3. Explain to a colleague what AI agents are and how they work (in plain English)
4. Identify 3 tasks in their daily work that AI can handle
5. Have 3 AI tools set up and working on their laptop before they leave the room

## 2. LEARNING OBJECTIVES (Bloom's Taxonomy)

By the end of this workshop, attendees will be able to:

1. **EXPLAIN** what AI is and what the major tools do, in plain English, to a colleague who wasn't at the workshop
2. **COMPOSE** effective prompts using the RACE framework (Role, Action, Context, Expectations) that produce useful outputs for their specific job tasks
3. **EVALUATE** AI-generated outputs for accuracy, usefulness, and appropriate use in a professional setting
4. **SET UP** three AI tools on their personal device: a conversational AI assistant, an AI writing tool, and one workflow automation
5. **IDENTIFY** at least three repetitive tasks in their current role that AI could handle, with estimated time savings
6. **CREATE** a personal AI action plan for implementing what they learned starting Monday morning

## 4. DETAILED SESSION PLAN

### OPENING (10 min) | 9:00 - 9:10

**Purpose:** Set the tone, establish trust, remove anxiety

**Key moments:**
- Brandon introduces himself as a practitioner, not a professor. "I run 5 businesses on AI. I'm going to show you exactly what I do."
- Acknowledge the elephant: "You're here because someone told you to learn AI, and you don't know where to start. That's normal. That's why we're here."
- Set expectations: "In the next 2 hours, I'm going to show you what AI can actually do for YOUR job. Not theory — real demos, real tools, hands-on."
- Quick show of hands: "Who has used ChatGPT before? Who uses it regularly? Who has never opened it?" (This calibrates the room and builds community — they see they're not alone.)

---

### BLOCK 1: "What AI Actually Is" (30 min) | 9:10 - 9:40

**Learning objective:** EXPLAIN what AI is; USE an AI tool successfully for the first time

**Key concepts (2 — keep it tight):**
1. AI is a prediction machine — not magic, not sentient. Just type what you need in plain English.
2. The Big 4 tools: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini. Quick overview — which to try first (Claude or ChatGPT, both free).

**Live demo (15 min):**
- Brandon opens Claude.ai on the projector
- Demo 1: "Summarize this 3-page report in 5 bullet points" — paste a real document, result in real time
- Demo 2: "Write a professional email declining a meeting" — show tone/context handling
- Demo 3: "Explain quarterly results like I'm new to finance" — show complexity adaptation
- After each: "No coding. No software install. Just English."

**Hands-on exercise (10 min):**
- Attendees open ChatGPT or Claude (Google sign-in, 90 seconds)
- "Ask it to do something useful from YOUR job this week. Here are starters if you're stuck:
  1. 'Summarize this email thread and list action items'
  2. 'Write a professional response to this email'
  3. 'Help me explain [work topic] to someone outside my department'"
- Brandon walks the room, helps anyone stuck, celebrates wins

**Quick debrief (5 min):**
- "Who got something useful?" — 2-3 people share
- Privacy basics in 60 seconds: don't paste confidential data, use "don't train" setting
- "AI works. You just proved it. Now let me show you how to make it 10x better."

**Expected outcome:** Every attendee has used AI successfully. Anxiety replaced with curiosity. They want more.

---

### BLOCK 2: "Prompt Engineering" (25 min) | 9:40 - 10:05

**Learning objective:** COMPOSE an effective prompt using the RACE framework

**Key concepts (2):**
1. The RACE framework: **R**ole, **A**ction, **C**ontext, **E**xpectations. The difference between garbage output and gold.
2. Before/after: show the SAME task done with a lazy prompt vs RACE. Dramatic difference.

**Live demo (10 min):**
- BAD prompt: "Write me an email." → generic garbage
- RACE prompt: "You are a senior project manager at a pharma company (R). Write an email to my team (A) about moving the Q3 deadline from June 15 to May 30 due to FDA feedback (C). Under 200 words, professional, 3 action items (E)." → exactly right
- Show iteration: "Make the tone warmer, these are people I've worked with for 5 years." → AI adjusts instantly

**Hands-on exercise (10 min):**
- Each attendee picks ONE real task from their job
- Write a RACE prompt on the handout, then type it in
- Brandon walks the room, gives feedback, helps refine

**Quick debrief (5 min):**
- "Who got a dramatically better result? Show us."
- Drop the key line: "AI doesn't replace people. People who use AI replace people who don't. You just joined the right side."
- "Now let me show you something that's going to blow your mind."

**Expected outcome:** They've felt the before/after. They know RACE works. They want to learn more — but we're intentionally NOT going deep. That's the paid workshop.
