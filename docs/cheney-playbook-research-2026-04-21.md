## The STE sales framework (his call playbook)

Used on every CEO call. Source: Koerner, AI Rabbit Holes, Builder's Growth Lab interviews.

### S — Strategy (5 min)
Position as *"We stay on top of how AI is reshaping your industry so you don't have to."* Name 1–2 specific AI shifts in the prospect's industry before the call. Do NOT get technical.

### T — Transformation (10 min — the money)
**The magic question:** *"Who were you about to hire or wish you could hire? What would they do? What would you pay them?"*

Let the prospect answer. Then do the math aloud:
> *"So you were about to hire a $5K/month assistant to handle [task]. Here's what we'd do instead — we'd build an AI system inside [their stack] that does [task] automatically. The system costs $200/month to run."*

Drop a technical breadcrumb then pull back:
> *"We'd probably set this up with a Claude workflow, connect it to your CRM via Zapier, dashboard the outputs. You don't need to know any of that — that's why you hire us."*

### E — Education (5 min)
The close-the-ROI line:
> *"Imagine we could squeeze 10% more productivity out of your existing 60 employees by training them properly. On a $20M business, that's $2M in output. We charge $15K/month — $180K/year. So before we even touch a workflow, the training alone pays for us 10 times over."*

### The close
After STE, silence. Then: *"Does this sound like something that would move the needle for you?"*

If yes: *"Great. There's a $15K/month retainer, quarterly minimum, cancelable after that. Month one we install the foundational dashboard and automate the first process. I'll send the one-page agreement after this call and we kick off Monday."*

**Quote (Koerner):** *"If they're on the phone, they want it already. Sell it to them."*

## What he says founders GET WRONG (direct quotes and themes)

1. **"Don't become a dev shop."** If you sell custom code, you have to build and maintain. Instead, coach clients' directors of ops / finance leads / sales managers to vibe-code solutions themselves. The client credits *you* for their new capability.

2. **"Stop assuming other people know more about AI than they do."** They don't. "If you're listening to this right now, you're in the 5% just because you're in this sphere."

3. **Don't overbuild on the call.** Drop a technical breadcrumb, then pull back. *"That's a whole conversation in itself. Let's park it and come back once we're working together."*

4. **Don't start cheap.** The $15K number wasn't a mistake — it was an anchor. If he'd said $3K, he'd have $3K retainers.

5. **"Jumping into AI tools before they have a plan"** — the #1 founder mistake per Builder's Growth Lab Ep #35 title. The fix implied: use the STE framework to design the engagement *before* touching any tools.
