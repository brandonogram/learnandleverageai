## What kills this plan

1. **Brandon not dialing.** The whole plan rests on 10 calls/week. If those stop, pipeline dies. Non-negotiable.
2. **Pricing slippage.** If the first "no" triggers a discount, the whole value anchor collapses. Hold firm.
3. **Scope creep on retainers.** Saying yes to custom builds outside the 4-tier scope turns LLAI into a dev shop (the #1 Cheney anti-pattern).
4. **Delivery over-promise.** Brandon can handle 3, maybe 4 retainers solo. Beyond that, quality breaks. Cap pipeline intake accordingly.

## What makes this plan succeed

1. **Brianna and Steve deliver testimonials in week 2.** These become the foundation of every future sales call.
2. **The $997 assessment becomes a commitment device.** Clients who pay $997 and get value are 10× more likely to convert to retainer than cold prospects.
3. **LinkedIn posts compound.** Week 1 posts have 10 views. Week 8 posts have 500. Week 12 posts bring inbound leads (Infinity-Pools-style).
4. **Brandon's TSAS/302PB/C2C case studies give him credibility no other Delaware AI consultant has.** Lead with them on every call.
