**Post-workshop survey (Typeform, sent 2 hours after workshop):**

| # | Field | Intelligence It Gives Us |
|---|-------|-------------------------|
| 1 | **AI Skill Self-Assessment AFTER (same 1-10)** | The AFTER score. Before/after gap = our proof of impact. "Average attendee went from 2.3 to 6.8" is our #1 marketing asset. |
| 2 | What was the most valuable thing you learned today? | (open text) — exact language to use in future marketing. Their words, not ours. |

| 4 | What should we add or change? | (open text) — curriculum improvement |
| 5 | How likely are you to recommend this? (NPS 1-10) | Under 7 = dig into why. Over 8 = referral machine. |
| 6 | Did you purchase anything today? | (dropdown: AI Starter Pack / Advanced Workshop / Corporate Training / Consulting / Nothing yet) — tracks conversion, identifies who to follow up with |

| 9 | Can we use your feedback as a testimonial? | (Yes / No) |
| 10 | Anything else you want to tell us? | (open text) — catches everything else |

- Objection data (survey Q7) — agents auto-craft follow-up sequences that address the exact reason they didn't buy
- Corporate pipeline (survey Q8) — auto-triggers outreach to the manager they named
- Channel ROI (registration Q14) — know exactly which $1 of marketing produced which $1 of revenue
