## What the transcripts actually show

### Chris Kerner's playbook (the $1K AI Assessment)

**The product:**
1. Business owner calls an AI voice agent ("Annie") — 20–30 min structured interview, 24/7, no calendar coordination
2. Transcript → Claude → report generated in Gamma
3. Report delivered in 48 hours
4. 30-min follow-up call walks them through it (this is the upsell conversation)

**The report contents (steal this verbatim):**
- Executive summary of pain points from the call
- Outcome promise ("reclaim 8 hours/week")
- Effort-vs-Impact matrix (find the quick wins)
- 3–5 recommended off-the-shelf tools with specific install steps
- 4-day Quick-Win Plan (day 1: install Fathom, done)
- **Upsell menu** (what's AFTER the quick wins)
- **Financial impact quantified** ("8 hrs × $100/hr = $3,200/mo saved minus $59/mo in tools") — **duplicate this as slide 1 AND last slide**, it's the hook

**Pricing journey — do NOT start cheap:**
- Free (2–3 for testimonials) → $200 → $500 → $1,000
- At $200, buyers said "how good can it be for $200?" and didn't act on recommendations
- At $1,000, buyers treat it seriously and convert to $3K–$5K upsells because the jump is "in the ballpark"
- **Starting at $200 shoots yourself in the foot.** A $200-to-$3,500 jump is too big. $1,000-to-$3,500 is normal.

**Call it "Assessment" not "Audit."** People hate being audited. They like being assessed.

**The upsell menu Chris actively sells (pick ONE bread-and-butter):**
| Upsell | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Process optimization (fix before automate) | $3–5K | Found in every single assessment |
| Zapier/Make automation build | $1–3K | Per workflow |
| Custom GPT / knowledge system | $3–5K | Recurring if per-project (e.g., per real-estate listing) |
| CRM setup (GHL) | $3–5K | For biz owners with no CRM |
| **Speed-to-lead AI agent** | **$3–5K** | **Chris's recommended #1 — revenue-tied ROI, easy math** |

**The "why you need one upsell" rule:** don't spread thin. Pick the one with revenue-tied ROI (not nebulous "save time"). Speed-to-lead is the winner because the math is obvious: "Your average deal is $11K, your close rate after tour is 40%. If this agent gets you one more tour a month, it's $11K. The build costs $5K."

**How Chris got his first customers (no audience required):**
1. **Free assessments for warm network** (local CEO peer group, 11–12 guys) → testimonials + upsells
2. **Local AI meetup on Luma/meetup.com** — you become the de facto expert just by organizing it
3. **Partner with well-connected local host** — Chris's realtor hosted a free AI event in her office, free lunch-and-learn → sign-ups
4. **Free 20–25 min presentation in someone else's office** — PURE value, no pitch — collect emails at door
5. **Door-knocking local businesses** to talk about AI (not sell) — "conversation about AI in 2026 is dramatically easier than selling insurance"
6. **Office hours at a co-working space** — sit there, answer AI questions, leads come to you
7. **Twitter/LinkedIn posts with specific tactical framework** (this exact transcript came from Chris publishing a "7 ways to sell AI services" post)

### John Cheney's playbook (the $15K–$25K/mo Fractional AI Officer retainer)

**Year 1 results:** $2.5M revenue, >50% net profit, 1 person for first 6 months (hit $1M solo), currently 5 FTEs, projecting $7M in year 2.

**The story beats that matter:**
- Vibe-coded an "AI IQ test" in 3 days, $400 total cost
- Launched Sunday — 150 people took the test, **ZERO purchased**
- Fixed a Stripe bug (5 min) but didn't wait — **immediately pivoted to direct outreach**
- Messaged 6 CEOs he knew on LinkedIn with a FOMO pitch ("I can measure your whole team's AI readiness and build a plan so you don't miss the boat")
- Call 1 → "How much?" → "$15K" → "Done" → $180K in 6 weeks

**The pivot that scaled (Fractional AI Officer):**
- Client said "I don't like one-time training; stick around"
- John made up "Fractional Chief AI Officer" on the spot: **$15K/mo retainer**
- Closed 3+ more at $10K–$25K/mo in the next couple weeks
- Shifted from hours-based pricing ("12–15 hr/mo at $10K") to **system-driven pricing** ("we install and manage the system ongoing") — harder to fire, higher retention

**Target profile (who actually buys):**
- **$10M–$30M revenue** businesses (under $10M, hard to justify)
- **No CTO** — painting, pool construction, masonry, wedding venues, Tony Robbins, Dean Graziosi
- CEO is the buyer. The fear is "if I miss the boat, it's my fault."
- Brandon's New Castle County, DE is full of these exact companies.

**The sales framework: STE** (use this on every CEO call)

- **S — Strategy:** "How will AI affect your industry? Your competitors? We stay on top of this for you."
- **T — Transformation:** "Who were you about to hire? For how much?" → "We'll build a system for $25/mo that does that job — plus these others." (Have them tell you what you should say.)
- **E — Education:** "What if we got 20% more productivity out of your existing 60 employees? 10% more revenue on your $30M? That's $3M. We charge $180K/year. No-brainer."

**Closing the ROI gap verbatim:**
> "You're doing $20M a year. What if I could increase that by 10%? That's an extra $2M. You paying me $180K a year is nothing."

**Delivery anti-pattern (do NOT become a dev shop):**
- "Teach a man to fish" — the client's director of ops should be vibe-coding solutions, not you
- John coaches them, they grind, they succeed, they credit him, they keep the retainer
- The retainer holds because there's always a new AI capability to teach next month

**Lead-gen for beginners:**
- Use **Manus AI** (or Claude with deep research) to pull CEOs in your target industry within your geography — name, phone, email
- **Just call them.** "There's no vibe-marketing shortcut."
- Post on personal FB and LinkedIn about what you're doing — a 300-person network = ~30 business owners
- Referral close rate is very high: "go for the close much, much faster" on referral calls
