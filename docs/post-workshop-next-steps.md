## REVISED LLAI STRATEGY

The workshop wasn't a failure — it was a pivot signal. The right framing:

**What happened:** 2 people showed up. Both had real problems. We have the tools to solve them.

**What this means:**
1. Large free workshops with cold audiences don't fill seats in this market
2. Small, intimate, high-touch sessions with warm contacts DO work
3. The products (InboxPilot, ContentBrief) may be a better initial revenue path than workshop upsells
4. Brianna/DEBCC is the distribution channel — serve her well, she brings the network

**Revised model:**
- Free workshops are **lead generation** for the products, not direct revenue
- Products (InboxPilot $49-79/mo, ContentBrief $49-199/mo) are **recurring revenue**
- Corporate training ($5K-10K/day) comes from **relationships**, not cold acquisition
- Brandon's time goes to: (1) building relationships, (2) teaching workshops, (3) nothing else — agents handle the rest
