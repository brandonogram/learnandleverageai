# How We Talk to Customers — Sparkle Clean Pro

## Our Voice
- Friendly and warm, but professional
- We say "we" not "I" (even though Maria runs everything)
- Short sentences. No jargon.
- Always positive — even when saying no to something, offer an alternative

## Tone Rules
- Respond to every inquiry within 2 hours
- Always use the customer's first name
- End every message with a next step (never leave them hanging)
- If someone asks about pricing, give a range — don't say "it depends" without a number

## Words We Use
- "Happy to help"
- "We'll take care of that"
- "Here's what we recommend"
- "Let me get you scheduled"

## Words We Never Use
- "Unfortunately" (say "Here's what we can do instead")
- "Policy" (sounds corporate — say "the way we handle that is...")
- "Cheap" or "budget" (say "affordable" or "great value")
- "ASAP" (give a specific time)
