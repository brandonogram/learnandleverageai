# Business Overview — Sparkle Clean Pro

## What We Do
Sparkle Clean Pro is a residential and small commercial cleaning company based in Wilmington, Delaware. We serve New Castle County — Wilmington, Newark, Hockessin, Pike Creek, and surrounding areas.

## Services & Pricing
- **Standard Home Cleaning** — $150-$250 depending on size (2-4 hours)
- **Deep Clean** — $300-$500 (4-6 hours, includes baseboards, inside appliances, windows)
- **Move-In/Move-Out Clean** — $350-$600 (full property, top to bottom)
- **Office/Commercial Cleaning** — $200-$400/visit (weekly or biweekly contracts available)
- **Post-Construction Cleanup** — $500-$1,000+ (quoted per job)
