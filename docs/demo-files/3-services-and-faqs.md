# Services & FAQs — Sparkle Clean Pro

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does a cleaning cost?**
Standard home cleaning runs $150-$250 depending on the size of your home. We do a free walkthrough or phone estimate before your first clean.

**Do I need to be home?**
Nope. Most of our regular clients give us a key or garage code. We lock up when we leave.

**Do you bring your own supplies?**
Yes, everything. Vacuums, mops, all cleaning products. If you have a preference for a specific product (eco-friendly, etc.), just let us know.

**How do I schedule?**
Call or text us at (302) 555-0147, or email sparkle@sparklecleanpro.com. We usually book 3-5 days out.

**Do you do one-time cleanings?**
Absolutely. We do one-time deep cleans, move-in/move-out cleans, and post-construction cleanup. No commitment required.

**What if I'm not happy with the clean?**
Call us within 24 hours and we'll come back and fix it. No charge. That's our guarantee.

**Are you insured?**
Yes. Fully licensed, bonded, and insured in the state of Delaware.

**Do you offer recurring service?**
Yes — weekly, biweekly, or monthly. Recurring clients get the same cleaner every visit and priority scheduling.

**What areas do you serve?**
All of New Castle County — Wilmington, Newark, Hockessin, Pike Creek, Bear, Middletown, and everywhere in between.

**Can I get a quote without a walkthrough?**
Yes. Tell us the number of bedrooms, bathrooms, and the general condition, and we'll give you a phone estimate. We confirm the final price after the first visit.
