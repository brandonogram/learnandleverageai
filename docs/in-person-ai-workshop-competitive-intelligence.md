# In-Person AI Workshop Business Model: Competitive Intelligence Report

**Date:** 2026-03-21
**Purpose:** Inform business model decision for LearnAndLeverageAI in-person workshop offering

---

## Executive Summary

The in-person AI training market is exploding in 2025-2026. Pricing ranges from $199 (half-day local workshops) to $75,000+ (enterprise custom engagements). The sweet spot for independent trainers targeting small business owners and non-technical professionals is **$497-$997 per seat for 1-2 day workshops with 15-25 attendees**, yielding $7,500-$25,000 per event. The most successful operators combine Meta ads, chamber/SBDC partnerships, and community building with clear upsell paths to retainers or agency services.

## 3. Chamber of Commerce / SBDC Partnership Models

### The Big Picture
This is a massive institutional channel. Key programs:

**Google + U.S. Chamber: Small Business B(AI)sics**
- Google.org providing $5 million to U.S. Chamber Foundation
- Goal: Train 40,000 small businesses over 3 years
- Distribution: 1,500+ state and local chamber partners
- Format: In-person workshops + digital hub + certification
- Pilot cities: Atlanta, Detroit, Houston (expanding 2026)
- Cost to attendees: **FREE**

**America's SBDC AI U**
- Funded by Google.org
- AI Clinics providing training + 1:1 coaching
- Goal: 100,000+ clients
- Cost: **FREE**

**Intuit "More with AI" Tour**
- Nationwide roadshow of half-day events
- Cities: Boulder, Los Angeles, Mountain View, New Haven
- Cost: **FREE**

### Implications for Independent Trainers
- **Threat:** Free institutional programs compete on price (you can't beat free)
- **Opportunity:** These programs are surface-level (foundational). The gap is in **implementation, customization, and hands-on building**
- **Partnership play:** Position yourself as the local expert who runs the deeper follow-up workshop after the free intro session
- **Example:** Alabama SBDC partnered with Selma-Dallas County Chamber + local foundations to host workshops -- this model can be replicated by independent trainers

### How to Partner
1. Contact your local chamber's program director
2. Propose a free intro session (builds list) with paid advanced workshop upsell
3. Offer chamber members a discount on paid workshops
4. Get listed in chamber event calendars and newsletters (free distribution)
5. Co-brand with chamber for credibility

## 5. Most In-Demand Topics (2025-2026)

Ranked by market demand and willingness to pay:

1. **AI Agents / Agentic Systems** -- Hottest topic. Autonomous systems, multi-agent workflows, CrewAI, LangChain. Enterprise and SMB both want this.
2. **Prompt Engineering** -- Still foundational but becoming table-stakes. Less premium pricing than agents.
3. **Vibe Coding / AI-Assisted App Building** -- Named Collins Dictionary word of the year 2025. Stanford offering courses. Massive demand from non-technical people wanting to build apps.
4. **AI Workflow Automation** -- n8n, Make, Zapier AI. Practical ROI stories sell this easily.
5. **AI for Marketing/Content** -- Copywriting, social media, SEO with AI. Broad demand but lower price tolerance.
6. **AI for Specific Industries** -- Real estate, contractors, legal, healthcare. Premium pricing when deeply relevant.
7. **AI Cybersecurity** -- Growing concern, especially for companies adopting AI.
8. **AI Strategy/Governance** -- Executive-level, highest per-engagement pricing.

## 7. What Format Works Best

| Format | Pros | Cons | Best For |
|--------|------|------|----------|
| **Half-Day (3-4 hrs)** | Low commitment, easy to fill, low venue cost | Limited depth, harder to justify premium pricing | Intro/foundational, $199-$497 |
| **Full-Day (6-8 hrs)** | Good depth, single-day commitment | Long for beginners, requires lunch/breaks management | Intermediate, $497-$997 |
| **2-Day Intensive** | Deep learning, transformation possible, premium pricing | Higher commitment barrier, higher venue costs | Advanced/implementation, $997-$2,500 |
| **Weekly Series (4-6 weeks)** | Builds habit, allows practice between sessions, community | Hard to maintain attendance, scheduling conflicts | Ongoing education, subscription model |
| **Monthly Membership** | Recurring revenue, community building | Requires constant content creation, churn management | Scaled model, $99-$199/mo |

**The consensus:** 2 days is the sweet spot for premium in-person workshops. "Keeping it to a maximum of two days most often makes it easier to market and sell." Half-day works well for intro/teaser sessions that upsell to longer engagements.

## 11. Key Takeaways for Brandon / LearnAndLeverageAI

### The Opportunity
1. **Massive underserved market:** Most business owners (especially contractors/blue-collar) have zero AI knowledge. Damian Player is right -- "talk to a boomer above 35 for 5 minutes."
2. **Free programs are foundational only:** Google/Chamber/SBDC programs teach basics. The gap is implementation and hands-on building.
3. **Local market advantage:** No one is doing this in most mid-size cities. First mover wins.
4. **Contractors are especially underserved:** No competitor is specifically targeting this niche with in-person training.

### Recommended Model
Based on competitive analysis:

**Tier 1 - Free Intro (List Builder)**
- Partner with local chamber of commerce or SBDC
- 90-minute "AI for Business Owners" intro session
- Free, builds email list, establishes credibility
- Upsell to paid workshop

**Tier 2 - Paid Workshop ($497/seat)**
- Half-day (4 hours) or full-day (6 hours)
- 15-25 attendees
- Hands-on: attendees leave with working AI tools configured for their business
- Venue: Coworking space or hotel conference room ($300-$800)
- Revenue per workshop: $7,455-$12,425
- Run 2-4x per month = $15K-$50K/month

**Tier 3 - Premium Intensive ($997/seat)**
- 2-day deep dive
- 10-15 attendees (premium, intimate)
- Custom AI automation system built for each business
- Revenue per event: $9,970-$14,955
- Run monthly = $10K-$15K/month additional

**Tier 4 - Corporate On-Site ($3,000-$5,000/day)**
- Go to the client
- Custom curriculum for their team
- 10-30 employees
- Upsell from workshop attendees who are business owners

**Tier 5 - Monthly Retainer ($2,000-$5,000/month)**
- Ongoing AI implementation support
- Convert workshop attendees to retainer clients
- 5-10 retainer clients = $10K-$50K/month recurring

### Revenue Projection (Conservative)
- 2 workshops/month at $497 x 20 seats = $19,880/month
- 1 corporate on-site/month at $3,500 = $3,500/month
- 3 retainer clients at $2,500/month = $7,500/month
- **Total: ~$30,880/month**

### Marketing Priority
1. **Chamber of Commerce partnerships** (free distribution, credibility) -- start here
2. **Meta ads** targeting local business owners 35-60 ($1,000-$2,000/month budget)
3. **LinkedIn content** establishing authority
4. **Referral program** (past attendees get $50-$100 for referrals)
