### 1. replypilot.dev — $10.18/yr (Cloudflare)
- Best overall name. "ReplyPilot" perfectly describes AI-powered email replies on autopilot
- The .com is taken (active product in Australia, expires Apr 2026 -- could become available)
- .app is also taken, but .dev is clean
- Strong brand: "Your AI co-pilot for email replies"

### 2. mailcrew.app — $12.18/yr (Cloudflare)
- "MailCrew" = a team of AI agents handling your mail
- Plays well for chambers: "Your email crew, powered by AI"
- .com taken since 1999 but .app is totally clear
- .dev also available as backup

### 3. draftpilot.dev — $10.18/yr (Cloudflare)
- "DraftPilot" = autopilot for drafting emails
- Emphasizes the draft/review workflow (draft, flag, auto-send)
- .com taken but .dev is clean
- Pairs with the product's core feature: AI-generated drafts

### 4. triagemail.app — $12.18/yr (Cloudflare)
- "TriageMail" = exactly what the product does (inbox triage)
- Self-explanatory name, no guessing needed
- .com taken since 2008 but .app is wide open
- Chambers would immediately understand this: "AI that triages your inbox"

### 5. inboxwise.app — $12.18/yr (Cloudflare)
- "InboxWise" = smart inbox management
- Clean, professional, easy to say
- Both .app and .dev available
- Good for the chamber market: sounds business-appropriate

### Honorable mentions:
