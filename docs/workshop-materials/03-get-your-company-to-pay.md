### Tips for getting a "yes":

1. **Be specific about your results.** Fill in the actual hours and dollar amounts from your ROI calculator worksheet. Real numbers are more persuasive than general claims.

2. **Frame it as a team investment, not a personal one.** Managers approve spending that benefits the department, not just one person.

3. **Mention competitors.** If you know competitors or peer companies are training on AI, mention it. Nobody wants to fall behind.

4. **Offer to organize it.** Volunteer to coordinate — it makes the manager's job easier and shows initiative.

5. **Timing matters.** Send this when professional development budgets are being planned (beginning of quarter) or when there's unspent budget at end of quarter.
