### Slide 17 — The RACE Framework

**TIMING:** 10:25

**SAY:**
RACE. Write this down. This is your new best friend. Four letters that will change every single interaction you have with AI.

R is for Role — tell AI who to be. What expertise should it bring to the table? "You are a senior project manager." "You are a financial analyst." "You are a professional editor." This sets the lens it uses.

A is for Action — what exactly do you want it to do? Not "help me" — specifically. "Write a quarterly summary." "Draft an email." "Analyze this data set."

C is for Context — what background information does it need to do a good job? The situation, the audience, the specifics. "For a Fortune 500 bank." "For my team of 12 in the Philadelphia office." "Based on Q3 results."

E is for Expectations — how should the output look? What's the tone? How long? What format? "Professional but not stiff." "Under 200 words." "Three bullet points with recommendations."

Four letters. Every time you write a prompt, run through RACE in your head. It takes 30 extra seconds. And it's the difference between getting garbage and getting gold.

**DO:** Write R-A-C-E on a whiteboard or flip chart if available. Point to each letter as you explain it. Keep the pace slow enough for people to absorb each one.
