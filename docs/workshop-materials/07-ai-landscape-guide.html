The AI Landscape

What Actually Matters Right Now — A Plain-English Guide
Learn & Leverage AI
AI in 2026 fits into four categories. You don't need to learn all of them — but you should know what exists so you can pick what's useful for your work.
1

You type a question or paste in text, and they give you a useful answer. Think of them as a smart colleague who's read everything on the internet.

Where to Start (This Week)

  1. Create a free ChatGPT account at chatgpt.com
  2. Set up Custom Instructions — tell it your role, industry, and how you like responses
  3. Use it for your next 5 tasks instead of Google or doing it manually
  4. Try Perplexity (perplexity.ai) for your next research question
  5. Pick one repetitive task you do weekly and ask ChatGPT: "How would I automate this?"

The Honest Truth

  • You don't need all of these tools. Most people need 2-3, tops.
  • AI won't replace you. But someone who uses AI will work faster than someone who doesn't.
  • The tools change every few months. What matters is learning how to work with AI, not memorizing buttons.
  • Start small. One tool, one task, this week.
