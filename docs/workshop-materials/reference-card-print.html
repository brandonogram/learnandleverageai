Brandon's Top 5 Prompt Templates

Copy these exactly.
Replace [brackets] with your info.

1 The Email Writer

You are a [your job title] at a [your industry] company. Write a professional email to [recipient] about [topic]. The tone should be [friendly / formal / direct]. Keep it under [number] words. Include [action items, deadlines, next steps].
Use for: Client updates, team announcements, status reports, follow-ups.

2 The Document Summarizer

I'm pasting a [report, email chain, article, policy doc] below. Summarize it in [number] bullet points. Each bullet should be one clear sentence. Highlight any action items, deadlines, or decisions. Write it so a busy [executive / manager] can get the key points in 30 seconds.

[Paste your document here]
Use for: Long emails, reports, meeting notes, policy documents, articles.

3 The Data Analyzer

You are a [job title] with expertise in [field]. I'm sharing [sales numbers, survey results, budget figures]. Analyze this data and give me: (1) The top 3 trends or patterns, (2) Anything unusual or concerning, (3) 2-3 recommended actions. Present as a brief executive summary (under 200 words) followed by a detail table.

[Paste your data here]
Use for: Spreadsheet data, quarterly numbers, survey results, KPIs.

4 The Meeting Prep

I have a meeting with [who] on [topic] on [date]. My goal is [what you want]. The audience cares most about [their priorities]. Prepare: (1) 5 talking points addressing their priorities, (2) 3 potential tough questions with responses, (3) A one-paragraph opening statement. Keep everything concise and practical.
Use for: Presentations, client calls, vendor negotiations, performance reviews.

5 The Weekly Planner

You are my executive assistant. Priorities this week: [list 3-5]. Recurring commitments: [list meetings/tasks]. Create a Mon-Fri daily plan that: blocks focused time, groups similar tasks, leaves buffer for surprises, and identifies what I could delegate or use AI for. Present as a simple daily schedule.
Use for: Sunday evening or Monday morning planning. Whole week organized in 2 min.
