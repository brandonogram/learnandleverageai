## For Everyone (Any Role)

### 1. Inbox Zero in 5 Minutes
> "I'm going to paste 3 emails. For each one, tell me: (1) what they want, (2) if I need to respond, (3) a draft reply if yes. Keep replies under 100 words and professional."

### 2. Meeting Prep in 60 Seconds
> "I have a meeting about [topic] in 30 minutes. Give me 5 smart questions to ask and 3 things I should know going in. My role is [your role] at a [your industry] company."

### 3. The Weekly Report You Never Want to Write
> "You are my writing assistant. I need a weekly status update for my manager. Here's what happened this week: [paste bullet points]. Turn this into a professional 200-word update with highlights, risks, and next steps."

---

## For Finance / Banking

### 4. Variance Analysis Explainer
> "You are a senior financial analyst. I'm going to paste budget vs. actuals data. Identify the top 3 variances, explain why each likely happened, and suggest one action item per variance. Format as a table."

### 5. Regulatory Summary
> "Summarize this regulatory update in 5 bullet points. Highlight what affects [our company/our department] specifically. Flag any deadlines or action items. Write it so a non-lawyer can understand it."

---

## For HR / People Ops

### 6. Job Description in 2 Minutes
> "Write a job description for a [role title] at a [industry] company in [location]. Include responsibilities, qualifications, and a brief culture statement. Keep it under 400 words and avoid jargon."

### 7. Performance Review Draft
> "Help me write a mid-year performance review for a direct report. Their strengths are [list]. Areas for growth are [list]. Keep the tone constructive and specific. 200 words max."

---

## For Marketing / Communications

### 8. First Draft of Anything
> "You are a senior marketing writer. Draft a [email/blog post/social post] about [topic]. Target audience: [who]. Tone: [professional/casual/urgent]. Length: [word count]. Include a clear call to action."

---

## For Operations / Project Management

### 9. Status Meeting Notes → Action Items
> "I'm going to paste my meeting notes. Extract all action items, assign them to the person mentioned, and add a suggested deadline for each. Format as a numbered list."

### 10. Process Documentation
> "I'm going to describe a process we do every week. Turn it into a step-by-step SOP (standard operating procedure) that a new hire could follow. Include tips for common mistakes."

---

## The Output Check (Do This Every Time)

AI is a first draft, not a final draft. Before using any output:

1. **Verify numbers.** AI can hallucinate statistics. Check any figures against your source data.
2. **Proof-read for tone.** Make sure it sounds like YOU, not a robot.
3. **Check for confidentiality.** Don't share AI output that includes info you wouldn't email to a stranger.
4. **Iterate.** If the first result isn't right, tell AI what to fix. "Make it shorter." "Change the tone." "Add more detail about X."
