## BLOCK 2: RACE Framework (Slides 11-13) | 6:35 - 6:50

---

### Slide 11 — The RACE Framework

**TIMING:** 6:35

**SAY:**
RACE. Four letters that change everything.

R — Role. Tell AI who to be. "You are a senior financial analyst." "You are an HR director with 15 years of experience." When you give AI a role, it writes like that person.

A — Action. Tell AI exactly what to do. Not "help me with my email." Instead: "Write a follow-up email to my client."

C — Context. Give AI the details. The more context you give, the better the output. Your company name, the situation, the audience, the goal.

E — Expectations. Tell AI the format and constraints. "Under 200 words. Professional tone. Include 3 bullet points." This is where most people miss — they don't tell AI what "good" looks like.

You use RACE, and AI goes from a mediocre intern to a senior colleague. I'm about to prove it to you.

**DO:** Point to each letter as you explain it. Go slower here — this is the core teaching moment.

**TRANSITION:** Let me show you the difference.
