## The 5-part call flow (10-15 min total)

### 1. Opener — pattern interrupt (15 sec)

> "Hey [Name], Brandon Calloway from Learn and Leverage AI — I know this is out of the blue, do you have 60 seconds?"

If yes → continue. If no → "Totally — best day/time for a 10-min callback this week?"

### 2. Specificity hook — prove you did homework (30 sec)

> "Quick reason I'm calling — I'm in Wilmington, I run [TSAS / 302 Photo Booth / Call2Calendar], and I spotted something specific about [their business] I wanted to run by you."

Then say the ONE specific thing from their dial sheet — the review complaint, the review praise, the seasonal-timing observation. NOT a generic compliment.

### 3. The question — Cheney's magic line (1-2 min)

> "Before I go into anything — **are you and your team using AI right now?**"

Listen. Shut up. They'll tell you.

Most likely answer: *"Not really. Some ChatGPT here and there."*

Your response:
> "Yeah, that's where 99% of [landscaping / veterinary / your-industry] owners in Delaware are. You're actually ahead of most just by asking. Here's what I do — I run the AI stack for three of my own Delaware businesses, and I help other NCC owners do the same. One 20-minute assessment, 48-hour custom report. Specific tools, specific hours saved, dollars per month. $997 flat, money-back if I don't find you 5+ hours/week."

### 4. The close — go for the booking NOW (1 min)

> "Does that sound like something that'd be useful?"

- **YES** → "Great. I'll text you a Stripe link the second we hang up. Want to go ahead and call my intake line when it's quiet — today or tomorrow? Number's (302) 416-6285. You pay, call the number, 20 minutes, report in your inbox Friday."
- **MAYBE** → "Totally fair. What specifically would need to be true for you to say yes?" (Listen. Answer it. Re-ask the close.)
- **NO / "send me info"** → "Cool — I'll email you a one-pager. If anything in there lines up, text me. If not, no worries." Move on. Do not nurture.

### 5. Wrap (15 sec)

> "Appreciate the time, [Name]. One way or another — if you ever want to see how an AI receptionist actually handles a call in real time, call the 302 number and play around. It's mine, it's the one your report comes from."

(That last line plants a demo in their pocket. They will call it out of curiosity. That's a lead.)

| "I can do this myself / my nephew's into AI" | "You probably can. Most of my clients can too. The question is how much of your week you want to spend figuring out which of 47 tools actually works. What's your hour worth?" |

## Rules

1. **Do not pitch the $4,997 retainer on the first call.** That's the post-assessment close, not cold-open material. $997 wedge first.
2. **Do not offer a discount.** Cheney rule. If they can't do $997, they can't do the retainer either.
3. **Do not extend past 15 minutes.** Book the next step or disqualify. No "next week" follow-ups unless a calendar invite is on the books.
4. **Take 30 seconds after every call** to write 2 lines into a call log: name, outcome, next step. (Below.)
