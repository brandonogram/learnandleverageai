## Codex's Key Strategic Recommendations

1. **Optimize for 8-12 high-fit buyers, not 25 random bodies.** Quality over quantity. 8 corporate managers who each spend $500+ is $4K+. Add 1 corporate training inquiry and you're at $10K+.

2. **Reframe as "invite-only AI working session"** instead of "workshop." More exclusive. Higher perceived value. Easier to fill with the right people.

3. **"Reply YES and I'll save your seat"** — remove all friction from personal outreach. Don't send people to a website. Register them manually.

4. **Phone calls beat texts for this demographic.** 45-60 year olds respond better to phone calls than text messages.

5. **Target HR/L&D managers, not just individual contributors.** One HR person who sends 5 team members is worth more than 50 cold outreach emails. And they're the ones who buy $5K-$10K corporate training.

6. **Focus on corporate-specific outcomes** in all messaging: drafting emails faster, summarizing meeting notes, creating slides, analyzing spreadsheets. NOT service-business demos (phone agents, automated scheduling).

7. **Book follow-up meetings BEFORE the workshop.** Don't rely on closing energy in the room. If Brandon has 3-4 "let me show you what this looks like for your department" meetings scheduled for the following week, those convert to corporate training deals.

8. **If under 8 confirmed attendees by Wednesday noon, pivot.** Either make it "invite-only pilot" or postpone to the following week. A half-empty room kills the upsell energy.

9. **Stop building assets and start making calls.** The plan has extensive "build" tasks (flyers, event listings, Reddit posts). These are low-conversion busywork. The ONLY thing that matters is Brandon personally inviting people he knows.
