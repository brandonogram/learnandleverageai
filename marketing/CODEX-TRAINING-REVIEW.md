# Codex Training Content Review — Final Recommendations

**Status:** COMPLETE. These changes should be made before Thursday.

---

## CRITICAL: Replace Call2Calendar as Centerpiece Demo

**Problem:** Call2Calendar is impressive but wrong for corporate professionals at JPMorgan/DuPont. Lines like "My customers don't even know they're talking to AI" trigger compliance/ethics alarms. "I don't have a receptionist, I have AI" triggers job-replacement fears.

**Fix:** Replace Block 3 "jaw-drop demo" with a corporate "AI Chief of Staff" demo:
- Input: messy meeting notes + quarterly update text
- Output: 6-bullet executive brief, 3 risks, 3 decisions needed, draft email to CFO, 5-slide outline
- Move Call2Calendar to a 90-second optional teaser at end or stay-after

## CRITICAL: "People who use AI replace people who don't" is RISKY

For an anxious 55-year-old audience, this line creates fear, not motivation.

**Fix:** Replace with: "People who learn to work with AI will outperform people who ignore it."

## Pacing Issues

- First exercise (10 min to sign up, invent a use case, get a win) is too aggressive for total beginners after work
- Only 18 min of true learner practice in a "hands-on" workshop — too thin
- "No break ensures 100% for the close" is wrong design principle for this audience

**Fixes:**
- Use ONE tool (Claude), not "Big 4" choice overload
- First exercise: fully guided with a PROVIDED sample + prompt to copy
- Add 5-minute stand/stretch break at ~55 min mark
- If 25 people, add a helper (or recruit Brianna)

## Corporate-Specific Exercises (Replace Generic Ones)

Three role-based tracks:
1. **Finance/Risk:** QBR summary, variance commentary, CFO email
2. **Operations/PM:** steering update, action log, stakeholder summary
3. **People/Leadership:** policy summary, manager FAQ, team announcement

Add printed sample docs so nobody has to paste real company content.

## Governance (Expand from 60 Seconds to 5 Minutes)

Add Green/Yellow/Red model:
- **Green:** Public or generic content
- **Yellow:** Anonymized internal content
- **Red:** Customer data, nonpublic financials, HR cases, regulated material

Add verification checklist: "Check numbers, names, nuance."

## Simplify the Close

Cut from 5 offers to 2 primary paths + enterprise conversations:
1. **Individual:** AI Starter Pack ($497) — personal implementation
2. **Team:** Corporate Training ($5K-$10K) — bring this to your organization
3. **Consulting:** By conversation only in stay-after, not on stage

Replace 10% urgency discount with useful bonus: "Get the role-based prompt pack" or "Bring one real work document to the paid lab."
