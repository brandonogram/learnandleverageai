# Target List v2 — Owner-Led Service Businesses (NCC / Delco / Chesco)
*Compiled: 2026-04-21*

## ICP Filter Applied

Owner-led service businesses in **New Castle County DE, Delaware County PA, or Chester County PA**. Roughly **10-50 employees**, no full-time CTO, operating pain visible (slow lead response, manual reports, dispatch chaos, repetitive customer comms). Priority industries: pool/aquatic, HVAC/plumbing/electrical, landscaping/lawn/tree, roofing/exteriors/GC, event/wedding venues, multi-provider vet/dental/family medical, niche manufacturing/fabrication, pest control + septic + disaster restoration.

**Excluded:** chains, national franchises, remote-owner operators, AI-mature shops, one-person shops, anything in Philly proper or NYC.

## How Sourced

Web-grounded research via Google Search + direct fetch of company About pages. Owner names were confirmed on one of: (a) the company's own About/Team page, (b) BBB business profile, (c) reputable trade press (Delaware Business Times, Construction Business Review, County Lines Magazine), or (d) LinkedIn company profile. Perplexity research was attempted but the API quota was exhausted, so verification was done via direct site fetches. Every row below has a named owner confirmed against a public source — I dropped every business where I could only verify the family name or a first name. Emails are listed only where actually published publicly; many owners only expose a contact form or a general `info@` inbox.

**Under-honest reporting:** I shipped **27 verified-owner rows** instead of 30. Three industries were thin: dental (only surfaced 1 clean match), event venues (most are single-owner boutique operations, not 10-50 employees), and niche manufacturing (most hits were too big or too small). I'd rather ship 27 real names than pad with three "family owned — call and ask for the manager" guesses.

**Phone numbers are the business main line** — only a few owners publish their direct cell. For high-priority leads, call the main line, ask for the owner by first name, and you're past the gatekeeper in one step because you're not cold-guessing.
