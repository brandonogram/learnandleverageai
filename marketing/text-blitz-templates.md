## IMPORTANT: The "Reply YES" Approach

**The #1 friction killer:** Don't make people visit a website to register. Just tell them to reply "YES" and you'll save their spot. Then Claude registers them manually in GHL.

**How it works:**

3. Brandon texts their reply to Claude (or just says "add [name] [email]")

## THE BEST APPROACH: "Results in Advance" (Hormozi Framework)

**Instead of just inviting people, GIVE THEM A FREE AI TIP FIRST.** This proves the workshop is worth attending before you even ask.

### Step 1 — Send this text (no ask, just value):
> Hey [name], quick AI tip: open ChatGPT and paste in your last long email. Type "Summarize in 3 bullet points with action items for me." Try it right now — takes 10 seconds.

### Step 2 — Send this 2 hours later:
> Did you try it? That's one of about 20 tricks I'm teaching Thursday evening at the Hilton in Newark. Free, 2 hours, small group. Reply YES if you want a seat.

**Why this is the best approach:** You proved the value BEFORE asking for anything. They experienced the result. Now attending the workshop is obvious. This converts at 2-3x the rate of a cold invite.

### Alternative Step 1 (for people you're less close with):
> Hey [name], have you tried using AI for [their specific work]? I've been running my businesses on it for a year. Quick tip: [specific tip related to their role]. Let me know if it helps.

### Then the invite in Step 2.

## ALSO POWERFUL: "The Private Invitation" Reframe

Instead of "free workshop" (sounds mass-market), say:
> [name] — I'm putting together a private AI working session for 10 professionals in the area. Thursday at the Hilton in Newark. Everyone brings their laptop, I walk you through the tools I use to run my businesses. You came to mind. Interested?

**Why this works:** Invitation > advertisement. Being selected is flattering. "Came to mind" creates belonging.
