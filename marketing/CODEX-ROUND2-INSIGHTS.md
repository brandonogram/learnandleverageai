## Round 2: Plan Challenge (b18vhxh2b)

### CRITICAL: Revenue Target is Fantasy

- "$20K+ sold Thursday evening" is not a plan — it's an upside outlier
- Realistic: 10-16 attendees, 2-4 decision-makers, 1-4 low-ticket sales, 1 serious follow-up booked
- Immediate cash: $1K-$4K
- 14-day pipeline: $5K-$15K
- $20K only happens if one pre-warmed corporate buyer is already in motion

### MESSAGING REFRAME (Agreed by both AIs)

**Stop saying:** "Free AI workshop"
**Start saying:** "Closed-door AI working session for managers, HR/ops, and professionals who want one live workflow built Thursday night"

**Stop saying:** "Only 10 seats left" (generic scarcity)
**Start saying:** "4 manager seats, 4 operator seats, 4 owner seats — assigned manually"

**Stop saying:** "Comment INTERESTED"
**Start saying:** "Text me SEAT" or "DM me SEAT"

### REAL URGENCY (Not Fake)

- "Send me the task you want fixed by 3 PM Wednesday if you want it built into Thursday night"
- "We start building at 6:10 PM sharp; after that, you're walking into the middle of the movie"
- "No replay. No slides. Laptop open."
- "Attend Thursday April 2, use it Monday April 6" (makes the date matter)

### MAKING MISSING IT FEEL EXPENSIVE (Not Begging)

- "If you're still doing first drafts, meeting summaries, and slide outlines manually in April 2026, you're choosing to stay slower than people around you."
- "If you manage people and still haven't seen AI used on a real work task, you're leading blind."
- "If AI is still on your 'someday' list, you're choosing slower output on purpose."

### OPTIMAL TEXT BLITZ (Better Than Plain Texts)

Best stack per Codex:
- 10 phone calls (highest conversion for 45-60 demo)
- 10 voice notes (personal, high trust)
- 20 texts (with "Reply YES" frictionless registration)
- 5 company-seat asks (to managers/HR)
- 3 intro-thread asks ("can you connect me with your HR/L&D person?")

### DEMOTE THESE CHANNELS (Time Wasters)

- 13 event calendar submissions → only Eventbrite (instant)
- New cold org emails → near-zero conversion
- Craigslist/Reddit → wrong audience
- Flyer-first networking → low conversion for this audience

### PROMOTE THESE CHANNELS

- Manager/HR cluster recruiting (one yes = 3-5 people)
- Pre-booked buyer meetings BEFORE the workshop
- Voice notes and phone calls (trust builders)

## Training Content Review (bjr02mosl)

### Corporate Device Access Problem

- Many JPMorgan/Capital One employees CANNOT access ChatGPT on corporate laptops (security policies)
- **Solution:** Ask attendees to bring PERSONAL devices (laptop or phone) in the pre-event text
- **Backup:** Have printed prompt examples that show what the output looks like

### Finance-Specific Demos Needed

- Current demos are generic. Need at least ONE that speaks to finance/pharma:
  - Variance analysis explanation
  - Policy/compliance document summary
  - Budget vs. actuals analysis
  - Regulatory filing summary

### "Monday at 8:30 AM" Takeaway

- Create a 1-page handout: "10 Things to Try When You Get to Work Tomorrow"
- Role-based prompts (finance, HR, marketing, operations, management)
- Immediately usable — no setup required

### Address Hallucination Fears

- Corporate professionals are TERRIFIED of AI making mistakes
- Add a quick "output check" section: "Always verify numbers. Always proof-read. AI is a first draft, not a final draft."
- Printed cheat sheet for fact-checking AI output

### Pair-Share Discussions

- Add 2-minute table discussions after each hands-on exercise
- "Turn to your neighbor and show them what you got"
- Builds community, reduces embarrassment, creates energy

### Block 2 Title

- "Prompt Engineering" still appears in some places — rename to "How to Talk to AI" everywhere
- Speaker guide MASTER TIMING table had old name (FIXED)
