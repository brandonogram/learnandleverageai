## DIAGNOSIS: Why We're at 2/25

**Hard truth:** 9 days of effort, $250 in ads, 558 landing page views = 2 registrations (0.36% CVR).

**Root causes:**
1. **Brandon's personal network hasn't been activated.** His LinkedIn/FB personal posts haven't been posted. Personal outreach to people he knows hasn't happened. This is the single highest-conversion channel and it's untouched.
2. **Landing page conversion is broken.** 558 people visited and 2 registered. A 3-5% CVR would have given us 17-28 registrations from the same traffic. Something on the page is killing conversions.
3. **Outreach to organizations produced 1 real reply from 33+ emails.** Cold email to orgs is low-conversion, especially with a 5-day lead time.
4. **No event listing platforms used.** Eventbrite, AllEvents, event calendars — none submitted. These feed Google Events and reach people actively looking for things to do.
5. **No direct personal invitations.** The highest-converting channel in existence (personal text/call from someone you know) hasn't been used at all.

**The fix:** We stop relying on cold traffic from ads and organizations. We go warm — personal network, referrals, partnerships, and walk-in-friendly tactics.

1. **Move the registration form above the fold.** Don't make people scroll to find how to sign up.
2. **Reduce form to name + email only.** Phone is optional. Every field costs conversions.

7. **Mobile-optimize the CTA.** Most people will see this from a text message link on their phone.
