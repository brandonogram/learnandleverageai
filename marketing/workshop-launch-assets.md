### Post 1 (Monday, March 24) — Specific AI Tip

Quick AI tip for anyone who works in an office:

Next time you get a long email or document you need to summarize, open Claude or ChatGPT and paste it in with this prompt:

"Summarize this in 3 bullet points. Include any action items for me. Keep it under 100 words."

That's it. Takes 10 seconds. The summary is usually better than the one you'd write yourself because AI doesn't skip details when it's tired at 3pm.

I do this 20+ times a day across my businesses. Reports, emails, contracts, meeting notes — it all gets summarized before I read the full thing. Saves me at least an hour a day.
