   - Hands-on projects, practicals, hackathons
   - Certification-focused

   - AI training, flexible format
   - Onsite or live online
   - Corporate digital skills training

## 5. LOCAL ADVERTISING LANDSCAPE

### Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Advertising

**Estimated Costs for Targeting 35-60yo Professionals in Delaware Valley:**

| Metric | Estimated Range | Notes |
|--------|----------------|-------|
| CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) | $15-$45 | Professional services/B2B runs $25-45 |
| CPC (cost per click) | $1.50-$6.00 | B2B audiences more expensive |
| Estimated Audience Size | 500,000-1,000,000 | 35-60, professional interests, in geo |
| Recommended Daily Budget | $30-$75/day | For testing; scale what works |
| Cost Per Lead (estimated) | $15-$40 | For workshop registration |
| Cost Per Attendee (estimated) | $40-$100 | Assuming 30-40% show rate |

**Best Targeting Options:**
- Job titles: Business Owner, CEO, Founder, Manager, Director, VP
- Interests: Small Business, Entrepreneurship, Business Management, Professional Development
- Behaviors: Small Business Owners (Facebook behavior category)
- Education: College graduates
- Income targeting: Top 10-25% household income
- Geo: 25-mile radius around Hockessin/West Chester/Main Line

**Seasonal Notes:** CPC peaks in November ($1.32 median) and drops in January ($0.85). Plan campaigns for Q1 or Q3 for lower costs.

## 6. STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS

### Highest-Opportunity Approach

1. **Start with Chamber Partnerships:** Offer free 45-minute "AI for Business" talks at NCC Chamber, Chester County Chamber, Greater West Chester Chamber, and Main Line Chamber. Convert attendees to paid full-day workshops.

2. **Venue Strategy:**
   - Free intro talks: Library meeting rooms or Chamber events (zero cost)
   - Paid half-day workshops: Align.Space West Chester ($300-500) or HeadRoom Media ($300-500)
   - Premium full-day workshops: Desmond Hotel Malvern ($84-99/person all-inclusive) or Hotel du Pont Wilmington (no sales tax)

3. **Advertising Stack:**
   - Meta ads targeting 35-60yo professionals within 25 miles of Malvern/West Chester ($50/day test budget)
   - Main Line Today advertisement (reaches exact target demo)
   - DELCO.Today newsletter sponsorship
   - LinkedIn ads for corporate team sales

4. **Community Flywheel:**
   - Speak at Rotary clubs (free, 6+ clubs identified)
   - Present at BNI chapters (3+ identified in target area)
   - Volunteer as SCORE workshop presenter
   - Attend/speak at Meetup groups (7 AI-focused groups identified)

5. **Corporate Sales Channel:**
   - Target Wilmington financial services companies (JPMorgan, Capital One, Barclays)
   - Target Route 202 pharma/tech corridor (Vanguard, Siemens, SAP)
   - Offer "team rate" workshops at the Desmond or Hotel du Pont

### Competitive Gap

**Nobody is doing what you're doing in this market.** The competition is either:
- Expensive university programs ($3,000-$15,000, multi-week)
- National training companies (generic, not tailored to SMBs)
- Online-only programs (no in-person community)
- Technical AI training (not practical business applications)

**Your positioning:** Practical, affordable, 1-day AI workshops specifically for small business owners and professionals in the Delaware Valley. Taught by someone local who understands the market.

- 25 attendees x $497 = $12,425 gross
