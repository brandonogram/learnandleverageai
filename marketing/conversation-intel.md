# Conversation Intelligence — What People Are Asking For

**Purpose:** Track what people say they want when they respond to outreach. This shapes future workshops, content, and offers.

## Format
| Date | Channel | Person | What They Asked/Want | Follow-up Needed |
|------|---------|--------|---------------------|------------------|

## Patterns to Watch For
- How many want **beginner** vs **advanced**?
- What specific **job functions** are interested? (HR, finance, operations, marketing?)
- What **companies** are they from?
- What **AI tasks** do they care about? (email, reports, automation, agents?)
- What **objections** come up? (time, relevance, "is this a sales pitch?")

## Notes
- Advanced interest → future $997 intensive or corporate training pipeline
- Job function data → shapes workshop exercises and examples
- Company data → helps target future corporate training outreach
