## Patterns to Watch For
- How many want **beginner** vs **advanced**?
- What specific **job functions** are interested? (HR, finance, operations, marketing?)
- What **companies** are they from?
- What **AI tasks** do they care about? (email, reports, automation, agents?)
- What **objections** come up? (time, relevance, "is this a sales pitch?")
