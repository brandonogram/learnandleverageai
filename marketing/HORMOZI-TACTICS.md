**Applied frameworks:** Value Equation, Grand Slam Offer, Core Four Leads, CLOSER, Scarcity/Urgency, Bonus Stacking

**Old positioning:** "Free AI workshop. Learn AI. Bring your laptop."

Your coworkers will think you went to a $5,000 conference. It costs you nothing."

## 5 NOVEL PLAYS

### Play A: "Results in Advance" (Text Blitz Enhancement)

**Step 1 (no ask):** Send a free AI tip via text/DM:
> "Hey [name], quick AI tip: open ChatGPT, paste your last long email, type 'Summarize in 3 bullet points with action items for me.' Try it. Takes 10 seconds."

**Step 2 (soft ask, 2 hours later):**
> "Did you try it? That's one of 20 tricks I'm teaching Thursday at the Hilton in Newark. Free, 2 hours. Want me to save you a seat?"

**Why it works:** Proves value before asking. The tip IS the lead magnet. Hormozi: "Give away the secrets, sell the implementation."

---

### Play B: "The Private Invitation" Reframe

Stop saying "free workshop." Instead:
> "I'm putting together a private AI working session for 10 senior professionals in New Castle County. Thursday at the Hilton. It's not a lecture — you bring your laptop and we build things together. Your name came up. Interested?"

**Why it works:** Invitation > advertisement. Status > free stuff. "Your name came up" creates belonging.

---

### Play C: "The Manager Ego Play"

Target managers specifically:
> "Hey [name], I know you manage [team] at [company]. I'm hosting a free AI training Thursday evening at the Hilton. Send 2-3 of your team — they'll come back Friday using AI tools. Makes you look forward-thinking. No cost to you or the company."

**Why it works:** Manager doesn't even have to attend. They get credit. Their team gets skilled. Zero budget impact.

---

### Play D: "The 60-Second Demo Video"

Brandon records a phone video showing ONE AI trick (e.g., paste long email → instant summary). Posts on LinkedIn:
> "This took 3 seconds. I used to spend 20 minutes on this. I'm teaching 20 tricks like this Thursday at the Hilton. Free. 10 seats. DM me."

**Why it works:** Video = 5x engagement. Showing > telling. Visual proof eliminates skepticism.

---

### Play E: "The Employer-Funded Pipeline"

Plant in every conversation:
> "If your company has a professional development budget, I also do corporate team training. The Thursday session is free and a good way to see if it's worth bringing to your org."

**Why it works:** Every free attendee becomes a potential $5K-$10K corporate training lead. The workshop is the "attraction offer" in Hormozi's Money Models.

## IN-ROOM UPSELL SCRIPT (CLOSER Framework)

### 8:00 PM — The Close (Last 10 Minutes Before Stay-After)

**CLARIFY** (throughout workshop): During exercises, ask each person what task eats their time. Write on whiteboard.

**LABEL:** "So half of you spend hours on email, three of you are drowning in reports, two of you manually update spreadsheets. That's hundreds of hours a year."

**OVERVIEW PAST PAIN:** "Raise your hand if you tried ChatGPT before and thought 'this doesn't work.'" (Hands up.) "That's because nobody showed you how to use it for YOUR work."

**SELL THE VACATION:** "Imagine walking into work tomorrow and finishing your first 3 hours in 45 minutes. You just did it in the last hour. Now imagine having 50 more prompts plus a 1-on-1 call with me to customize everything for your role."

**THE OFFER:**
"Here's what I have for people who want to go deeper:

**Option 1 — AI Starter Pack ($497)**
Everything we covered tonight plus 50 custom prompts for your job function and a 30-minute 1-on-1 call with me. You'll have a complete AI toolkit by next week.

**Option 2 — Advanced 2-Day Intensive ($997)**
I sit down with a group of 10 people for a full day and we build your complete AI system. Custom to your role. This is capped at 10 people and doesn't happen often.

**Option 3 — If you're a manager or business owner...**
I do corporate team training. I come to your office and train your whole team. Starting at $5,000 for a full day. If you're interested, talk to me during the stay-after — I'll walk you through what that looks like."

**BONUS (reveal AFTER the offer):**
"But for everyone in this room tonight — just tonight — I'm including the Advanced Prompt Library. That's 50 additional prompts I use to run my businesses. I sell it separately for $197. Free with any purchase made tonight."

**EXPLAIN CONCERNS (AAA):**
- "I need to think about it" → "Totally fair. What specifically? The $497? The time commitment? Let me address that."
- "My company should pay for this" → "Great idea. I have a template you can send your manager tonight. But the tonight-only bonus expires — grab it now, get reimbursed later."

**REINFORCE:** QR codes on every table. Stripe payment links on the handout. "Take out your phone. Scan. 30 seconds."
